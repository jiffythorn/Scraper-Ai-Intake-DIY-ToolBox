### The bundle math (why $29.99, not $45)

Buying separately = $44.97. Your instinct of "$45 for the package" prices
the bundle at **0% discount** — there's no reason to buy it. Bundles sell
when the discount is visible and honest:

- **$29.99 = 33% off** — the classic bundle sweet spot ("buy 2, get the
  3rd free"-shaped). Launch price.
- **$34.99 = 22% off** — steady-state after the launch window.
- Anchor line on the listing: *"Bought separately $44.97."*
- Don't go under $24.99 — it reads as "the cheap junk bin" and undercuts
  your services funnel.

**Optional ladder** (test after 20 sales): $29.99 bundle / $9.99 upsell
"add the complete toolbox" shown post-purchase to single-product buyers.

### Launch window (2 weeks)

- Bundle at $24.99 for the first 50 buyers ("founding 50"), then $29.99.
  Real scarcity, real deadline, no fake countdown timers.

## Compliance notes for the listings (keep these — they're your armor)

- No income claims, no "guaranteed leads," no results promises — the
  no-guarantees stance is already written into every doc; keep listings
  consistent with it.
- Keep the fine-print line on every listing ("You are the data
  controller..."). It converts *better* with sophisticated buyers and it
  matches the TERMS they accept at purchase.
- The products are MIT — buyers may resell with attribution. Say so
  proudly; it's a feature, not a leak. Your margins are in custom builds.
